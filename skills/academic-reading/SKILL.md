# Academic Paper Reading Workflow

## Core Process
1. **First Pass (5 min)**: Read title, abstract, introduction, conclusions. Identify the main contribution.
2. **Second Pass (30 min)**: Read methods, experiments, figures. Understand the technical approach.
3. **Third Pass (Deep)**: Reconstruct the work mentally. Question every claim. Identify assumptions.

## Critical Reading Checklist
- What problem does this solve, and is it important?
- What is the key insight or innovation?
- Are the experiments convincing? (datasets, baselines, metrics, ablation, statistics)
- What assumptions does the method make?
- What are the honest limitations?
- Could you reproduce this from the paper alone?

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid
- Don't accept claims at face value — verify against data
- Don't confuse correlation with causation in experiments
- Check if ablation studies actually support the claimed contributions
- Look for cherry-picked baselines or unfair comparisons
